# Capabilities and entities

- **Script:** `S/name-capabilities-and-entities.ts`. Assigning jobs, `registry/assign-units`, give every entry point, resource and data shape one entry; the first job of each registry runs alone, then the rest, each writing the entry a unit needs where none fits, each closed batch adding what they wrote for the next; every job is reviewed. Once every assigning job is decided, `registry/propose-entries` runs again in slices to merge the duplicates, each slice reviewed; then the stage writes once. Nothing waits on a job but its own outcome. Each job reads its own brief: its commands, the rules of its kind and what to read besides.
- **At done:** the run studies every capability (`--all`) unless the person names some; with none to study, go to Publish. The entry points no capability holds, the saved resources no kind of data holds and the entries the merge did not compare are reported by `show`; `show` also counts the signs of the grain (capabilities touching no data, the spread of entry points per capability) for you to read.
