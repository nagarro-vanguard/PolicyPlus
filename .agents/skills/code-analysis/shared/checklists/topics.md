# Review checklist: Topic Answers

Each answer is the headline of one topic: one subject, one question, read by a person who will not open the facts beneath. It claims what is true of the subject as a whole, and rests on every fact beneath, which the gate checks. Judge it against the facts beneath its topic only: it may rest on no other (the gate refuses it). Where a fact of another topic contradicts one beneath, never ask the answer to cite it: name the contradiction in your explanation.

## Wrong

- An answer a fact beneath contradicts, or one that says more than the facts it rests on (a number, a name, a scope none of them states).
- An answer that blends facts that disagree instead of saying they disagree and which to believe.
- An answer true of one part or one case stated of the whole subject, or the parts that differ left unnamed.
- An answer that drops a condition the facts beneath state, or merges a harm in that a finding already tells.
- An `assumed` answer the facts beneath do settle.
- Two harms written as one, or one harm told twice; a finding placed under a harm it does not tell; a severity the harm's worst place does not warrant.

## Missing

- Where the facts beneath are an area's notes, a part of the subject the answer drops: every area's notes are the part's whole.
- A disagreement between the facts beneath that the answer does not name.
