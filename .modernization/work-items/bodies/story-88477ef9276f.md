# Choose the products a filter requires

## Story

As a person using the product, I want in the filter dialog a person ticks Supported on to use the product requirement box, and ticks products, each tick spreading to the products beneath it., so that administrators narrow the policy list to the policies that apply to the Windows versions and products they manage..

## Context

This ports behaviour of the existing product, described in the attachment: 2 rules it must keep and the reading of the code behind them.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1: The product list and the two matching options cannot.**
Given the filter dialog has just opened, when Supported on is not ticked, then the product list and the two matching options cannot be used; when it is ticked they can be used.

**AC-2: Every product beneath it is ticked or unticked to.**
Given Supported on is ticked, when a person ticks or unticks a product by hand, then every product beneath it is ticked or unticked to match, and changes the dialog makes itself do not spread.

**AC-3: The list looks unusable and readable.**
Given Supported on is not ticked, when the dialog shows the product list, then the list looks unusable and readable.

## Terminologies

- Supported on: The filter condition that keeps policies supported by the chosen products; it is not the Policy type or Current state condition.
- Product requirement box: The part of the filter dialog holding the product list and the two matching options; it is not the whole dialog.

## Out of scope

The other tasks of this part of the product have their own stories: Apply the options chosen in the filter dialog; Open, reset or cancel the filter dialog.
