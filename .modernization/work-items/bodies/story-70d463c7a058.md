# Show category and policy details and keep the list tidy

## Problem

- People browse the definitions by what the pane tells them.

## Goal

- Rules for what the information pane, icons and state column show, and how selection survives a rebuild.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a selected or highlighted category, when the pane shows it, then it gives the title, "This category contains N policies and M subcategories." ("The selected category" when only highlighted) and its explanation, counting hidden items too.
- Given a selected policy, when the pane shows it, then it gives the title, "(no requirements information)" or "Requirements:" plus the support name, its explanation, and a preference notice only for preferences.
- Given a policy description, when it is shown, then leading and trailing spaces of every line are removed and trailing blank space is dropped.
- Given a policy, when its icon is chosen, then it is preference first, plain when it has no configurable elements, else "extra configuration".
- Given both areas shown, when a policy's state is displayed, then a policy applying to one area shows its state followed by "(C)" or "(U)"; with a single area only the state is shown.
- Given the category tree rebuilt from loaded definitions, when it finishes, then nothing is selected and the pane shows the empty state.
- Given a rebuilt list, when the earlier policy is still in the category shown and passes the visibility rule, then it is selected and revealed, otherwise nothing is selected; the earlier top row is restored if the policy is in the same category and the list has that many rows.

## Scope

- packages/tooling (the Tooling layer)
- packages/domain (the Domain layer)
- packages/contracts (the Contracts layer)
- packages/data (the Data layer)
- apps/api (the Services layer)
- packages/ui (the Components layer)
- apps/web (the Screens layer)
- apps/e2e (the Black-box tests layer)

## Out of scope

- Anything a later story builds on top of this.
