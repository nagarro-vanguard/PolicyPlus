# Browse products, their versions and details in one view

## Story

As a person using the product, I want show every loaded product with its number of children, open a product to see its major and minor versions together, and read the details of any product, version, child or parent in place., so that people can see a whole product family and read each item without narrowing list by list..

## Context

This ports behaviour of the existing product, described in the attachment: 9 rules it must keep and the reading of the code behind them.

Open points, to settle before it is built:
- What happens when the person chooses the parent button: are the parent's details shown in the same window?
- What happens when a product is selected and the person double-clicks it or presses Enter: are its details shown?

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1: Every loaded product is listed ordered by name, each.**
Given definitions are loaded, when the person opens the product browser, then every loaded product is listed ordered by name, each with its number of children.

**AC-2: They are the children of that product ordered by.**
Given a product is selected, when the major versions are shown, then they are the children of that product ordered by version, under a heading naming the product; with none selected the list is empty and asks the person to select one.

**AC-3: They are the children of that major version ordered.**
Given a major version is selected, when the minor versions are shown, then they are the children of that major version ordered by version, under a heading naming it; with none selected the list is empty and asks the person to select one.

**AC-4: Nothing happens.**
Given no product is selected in a list, when the person double-clicks or presses Enter, then nothing happens.

**AC-5: Every child is listed with its version and name;.**
Given a product with children is shown, when its details are read, then every child is listed with its version and name; a product with no children shows an empty list.

**AC-6: That child's details replace the current ones in the.**
Given a child is listed in the details, when the person double-clicks it, then that child's details replace the current ones in the same view; with nothing selected, nothing happens.

**AC-7: The parent name is shown and the button to.**
Given a product has a parent, when its details are shown, then the parent name is shown and the button to go to the parent is enabled; given it has none, the parent is blank and the button is disabled.

**AC-8: Its version number is shown; given a top-level product.**
Given a major or minor version is shown, then its version number is shown; given a top-level product is shown, then the version is blank.

**AC-9: It is labelled "Major revision", "Minor revision" or "Top-level.**
Given a product is shown, then it is labelled "Major revision", "Minor revision" or "Top-level product" according to its type.

## Terminologies

- Product family: A product together with its major and minor versions; not a single version on its own.
