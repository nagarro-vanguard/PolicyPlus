# Prove the journey: Find the support rules and see which products they...

## Problem

- the journey keeps working as the product changes, in the interface the team ships

## Goal

- an acceptance test that does the journey from its first step to its last, as people will do it

## Acceptance criteria

- Given the product is ready, when the test does "Say which product or name I care about.", then narrows support definitions to names containing the text, ignoring case, and to those covering the product; empty shows all, ordered by name ignoring leading spaces..
- Given the product is ready, when the test does "Open a support definition or rule and see which products it covers.", then shows its details, doing nothing when none is chosen..
- Given the product is ready, when the test does "Follow an entry to its product or nested definition.", then opens a product entry's product, shows a nested definition in the same place; missing product or nothing chosen does nothing..

## Scope

- packages/contracts (the Contracts layer)
- packages/domain (the Domain layer)
- packages/database (the Database layer)
- apps/api (the API service layer)
- packages/ui (the UI components layer)
- apps/web (the Web app layer)
- apps/e2e (the End-to-end tests layer)
- packages/tooling (the Tooling layer)

## Out of scope

- Anything a later story builds on top of this.
