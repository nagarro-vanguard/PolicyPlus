# Show the SPOL fragment with an optional header

## Problem

- People can copy a fragment alone or a complete SPOL document.

## Goal

- Show a policy's SPOL fragment with the header only when asked.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given the fragment is shown, when 'Include SPOL header' is ticked, then the text starts with 'Policy Plus Semantic Policy' and a blank line; otherwise only the fragment appears.

## Scope

- packages/tooling (the Tooling layer)
- packages/domain (the Domain layer)
- packages/contracts (the Contracts layer)
- packages/data (the Data layer)
- apps/api (the Services layer)
- packages/ui (the Components layer)
- apps/web (the Screens layer)
- apps/e2e (the Black-box tests layer)

## Out of scope

- Anything a later story builds on top of this.
