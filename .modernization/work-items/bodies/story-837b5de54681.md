# Open category and support rule details from policy details

## Story

As a person using the product, I want a person opens the details of a policy's support rule or category from the policy details window., so that they can see where a policy applies and what it needs..

## Context

This ports behaviour of the existing product, described in the attachment: 0 rules it must keep and the reading of the code behind them.

It relies on behaviour that other stories build first: Describe products and support definitions in their detail views; Open details of a policy, category, product or support rule.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1: The support rule's details open.**
Given a policy's details, when the person presses the support button, then the support rule's details open.

**AC-2: The category's details open.**
Given a policy's details, when the person presses the category button, then the category's details open.
