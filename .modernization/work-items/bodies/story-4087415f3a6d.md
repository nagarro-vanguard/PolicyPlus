# Work out categories, products, policies...: Definition structure builder

## Story

As a person using the product, I want from the validated templates, work out the categories, products, support rules, policies, their options and the screen layouts for entering values, ahead of use, with names and descriptions in the language., so that the person browses a ready, consistent set of categories and policies, where each policy shows what it applies to and how its options behave..

## Context

This ports behaviour of the existing product, described in the attachment: 13 rules it must keep and the reading of the code behind them.

It relies on behaviour that other stories build first: Check every template is sound before using it; Find each template and its text in the person's...: Language text reader; Work out categories, products, policies and the...: Policy option reader.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1: One with a named parent is attached under it,.**
Given categories, when they are built, then one with a named parent is attached under it, found in this load or earlier ones; if the parent is not found it has no parent but is still listed among the top-level categories.

**AC-2: It takes its category and 'supported on' rule from.**
Given a policy, when it is built, then it takes its category and 'supported on' rule from the references in its own content and has options only when it contains an options section; it is listed under its named category when found, and otherwise stays in the policy list with no category; it shows the 'supported on' rule its reference names when found, and otherwise none.

**AC-3: A major or minor version is attached under its.**
Given products, when they are built, then a major or minor version is attached under its parent product defined in the same template, and only products without a parent are top-level.

**AC-4: It is 'any of' when its first logic element.**
Given a support rule, when it is built, then it is 'any of' when its first logic element is "or", 'all of' when "and", and blank otherwise; only that first element is read, each reference or range becomes an entry and a range may carry a minimum and maximum version; an entry is linked to the product it names, else to the support rule of that name, else stays unlinked.

**AC-5: It applies to computers for "Machine", to users for.**
Given a policy class, when it is built, then it applies to computers for "Machine", to users for "User", and to both for any other value; a policy applies to an area when its definition declares it, and one declared for both applies to each.

**AC-6: It is a preference, not a managed policy, when.**
Given a policy's registry key, when its kind is decided, then it is a preference, not a managed policy, when the key is outside the three policy locations ("software, policies", "software, microsoft, windows, currentversion, policies" and "system, currentcontrolset, policies"), and a key counts as a policy key when it is one of those three or lies below one, regardless of case.

**AC-7: A template and its language file loaded together stage.**
Given loaded templates, when they are staged and built, then a template and its language file loaded together stage their categories, products, support rules and policies and are recorded under the template's namespace; when the structures are built the new items join the final lists and staged items are cleared, so loading more files adds to what is available.

## Terminologies

- Preference: A setting whose registry key lies outside the three policy locations; not a managed policy.
- Support rule: The statement of which products a policy works with; not the products themselves.
