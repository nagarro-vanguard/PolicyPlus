## Terminologies

| Term | Change | Today | Proposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Area | new | none: new in this codebase | The part of the policies being read: user policies, computer policies or both; it is not the place a policy is saved. |
| Empty category | new | none: new in this codebase | A category that holds no visible policy and no visible subcategory; it is not a category with no policies at all. |
| Focus | new | none: new in this codebase | The single category or policy whose description is shown; it is not a record of both a highlighted policy and a highlighted category. |
| Parent row | new | none: new in this codebase | The first row of a category's contents, marked Parent, that leads up to the category above it. |
| Item menu | new | none: new in this codebase | The right-click menu of a category or policy, offering only the actions that fit it; it is not a menu for the whole screen. |
