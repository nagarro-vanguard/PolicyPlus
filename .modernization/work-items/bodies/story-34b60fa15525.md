# Pick a search hit to open and step to the next hit

## Story

As a person using the product, I want from the hits, the person picks one to open its editor in the current area with the policy list moving to it, or moves on to the next hit still shown., so that picking is the point of the search, and stepping through hits is how a list of hits is worked..

## Context

This ports behaviour of the existing product, described in the attachment: 4 rules it must keep and the reading of the code behind them.

It relies on behaviour that other stories build first: Offer the area choice and reopen the last search results; Open the policy dialog and finish it with OK, Apply or Cancel; Open the chosen policy sources and keep the view on a visible category; Show each policy's state and comment and apply the filters.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1: That policy is passed back and its position in.**
Given a hit is selected, when the person presses Go or double-clicks it, then that policy is passed back and its position in the results is remembered; with no hit selected nothing is accepted.

**AC-2: The policy's editor opens in the current area and.**
Given the person picks a hit, when the results close, then the policy's editor opens in the current area and the policy list moves to that policy.

**AC-3: The hit after the last one chosen that is.**
Given a hit was chosen earlier, when the person asks for the next one (Find Next), then the hit after the last one chosen that is currently shown is taken, skipping hidden ones; when none remains they are told "There are no more results that match the filter."

## Out of scope

The other tasks of this part of the product have their own stories: Watch search hits arrive and stop the search when enough is found.
