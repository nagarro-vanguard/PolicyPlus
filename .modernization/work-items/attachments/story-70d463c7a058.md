## What each criterion must keep

### AC-1

- For a selected or highlighted category the pane shows its title, "This category contains N policies and M subcategories." ("The selected category" when highlighted but not opened) and its explanation. The counts include every policy and subcategory, hidden or not. (PolicyPlus/Main.vb:158-163)

### AC-2

- For a selected policy the information pane shows its title, "(no requirements information)" when it has no support rule or "Requirements:" plus the support name, and its explanation. A notice that the policy is a preference appears only for preferences. (PolicyPlus/Main.vb:149-157)

### AC-3

- The text of a policy description is tidied for display: leading and trailing spaces are removed from every line and blank space at the end of the text is dropped. (PolicyPlus/Main.vb:863-870)

### AC-4

- A policy's icon is chosen in this order: preference, then plain when it has no configurable elements, then "extra configuration" when it has some. (PolicyPlus/Main.vb:186-192)

### AC-5

- With both areas shown, a policy that applies to one area shows that area's state followed by "(C)" for computer or "(U)" for user. With a single area shown, only that area's state is shown, without a suffix. (PolicyPlus/Main.vb:248-252)

### AC-6

- After the category tree is rebuilt from the loaded definitions, no category, policy or highlighted item is selected and the information pane shows the empty state. (PolicyPlus/Main.vb:100-103)

### AC-7

- When the list is rebuilt, the policy already selected is selected again and revealed only if it is still listed, that is, it sits in the category shown and passes the visibility rule; otherwise nothing is selected. Then, if the policy is in the same category and the list still has that many rows, the earlier top row is put back, so the policy is not guaranteed to stay in view. (PolicyPlus/Main.vb:109-112)
