## What each criterion must keep

### AC-1

- A category's icon is chosen in this order: orphaned, then empty, then normal. (PolicyPlus/Main.vb:177-183)

### AC-2

- A category is visible when empty categories are switched on. Otherwise it is visible only if it has at least one visible policy or one visible subcategory. (PolicyPlus/Main.vb:196-200)

### AC-3

- A category appears in the tree only when it passes the visibility rule; each shown category gets an icon by its kind and carries its visible subcategories below it, and the tree is sorted. (PolicyPlus/Main.vb:90-95)

### AC-4

- The category tree selection follows the category shown in the right pane. (PolicyPlus/Main.vb:139-141)

### AC-5

- After the category tree is rebuilt from the loaded definitions, no category, policy or highlighted item is selected and the information pane shows the empty state. (PolicyPlus/Main.vb:100-103)

### AC-6

- Selecting a category in the tree makes it the current category and clears any selected policy. (PolicyPlus/Main.vb:482-485)

### AC-7

- The "Empty Categories" menu switches empty categories on or off and the view moves to the nearest visible category. (PolicyPlus/Main.vb:545-547)

### AC-8

- The area choice "User" shows user policies, "Computer" shows computer policies and any other choice shows both. (PolicyPlus/Main.vb:551-558)

### AC-9

- When the list is rebuilt, the policy already selected is selected again and revealed only if it is still listed, that is, it sits in the category shown and passes the visibility rule; otherwise nothing is selected. Then, if the policy is in the same category and the list still has that many rows, the earlier top row is put back, so the policy is not guaranteed to stay in view. (PolicyPlus/Main.vb:109-112)
