Overview › What can go wrong › Medium

Applying a policy, from the settings window or a script, clears its stored values or comment before the new ones are read or written. A failure, such as a drop-down with no choice, leaves the policy empty or half set and reports little.

Severity: Medium. Kind of risk: Data can end up wrong, lost or inconsistent.
