Overview › What can go wrong › Medium

Saving reports its result poorly. One handler covers both sources without saying which failed, success is shown even when nothing was writable, and the result of the Group Policy refresh request is ignored, so a person may be told a policy applied when it did not.

Severity: Medium. Kind of risk: Failures are hidden, lost or not retried.
