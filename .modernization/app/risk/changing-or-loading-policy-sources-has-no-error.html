Overview › What can go wrong › Low

Changing or loading policy sources has no error handling. Sources are replaced one by one, so a failure leaves a mix or an empty workspace with an unhandled error. A start-up fallback still presets the failed sources, and a missing registry location stops a POL export.

Severity: Low. Kind of risk: Failures are hidden, lost or not retried.
