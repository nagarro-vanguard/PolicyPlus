Overview › What can go wrong › Medium

A failed load of policy templates leaves the workspace inconsistent. Staged items survive and make later loads fail, partly filled lists make retries fail on duplicate names, and a download reloads after clearing the workspace, so a failure leaves it empty. Several actions reset it; others do not.

Severity: Medium. Kind of risk: Data can end up wrong, lost or inconsistent.

What the code shows (3)

A failed build of the policy lists leaves the workspace inconsistent. Staged items survive and are reprocessed on the next load, and a build that fails partway through the final lists leaves some filled, so a retry fails on duplicate names. Close workspace, Acquire, Open folder with clearing, and language reload reset it; failed Open folder and Open file do not, and only start-up resets automatically.
