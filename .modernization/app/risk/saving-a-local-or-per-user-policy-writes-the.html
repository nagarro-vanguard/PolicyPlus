Overview › What can go wrong › Medium

Saving a local or per-user policy writes the policy file first and the version file second. A failure on the second step, or a malformed version line that truncates the version file, leaves new settings with an old or damaged version, which Group Policy clients use to detect change.

Severity: Medium. Kind of risk: Data can end up wrong, lost or inconsistent.
