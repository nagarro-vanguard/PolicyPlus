Find policies whose title

Find policies whose title, description or comments contain words or phrases the person remembers.

2 steps today → 2 proposed7 rules kept · 0 dropped1 action droppedFinding policiesProposed

Today, read from the codeModernized: Search as you type

1Describe the words, wildcards or phrases to look for and which attributes to search.

Refuses empty text or no chosen attribute, splits the text into words, wildcard words and quoted phrases, and builds a test that matches a policy when any chosen attribute contains all of them; an unclosed phrase is silently dropped.

2Start the search or abandon it.

Starts a search with the entered terms, including comments of both areas, or closes without searching.

mergedSay the words or phrases remembered.Inferred

No separate action to start, and the common case needs no settings.

deferredNarrow where the words are looked for, if wanted.

Asked only when the person wants to narrow.

Why this way recommended

The system looks in all three places as the words are given, and narrowing the places is an option.

The usual search is one step and the person learns about an unclosed quote instead of silently getting wider results.

Trade-off: Searching on every change does more work and may show results for half-typed words.

The architecture, modernized

Rules kept and dropped: 7 kept, 0 dropped
  • Search text is split at spaces into plain words, wildcard words (containing "*" or "?") and quoted phrases.
  • A text is a hit only when it contains every plain word as a whole word, every wildcard matching some word, and every quoted phrase as whole words together.
  • A policy matches when the ticked attributes hit: its title, its description, or a comment on it from any supplied comment source (user or computer).
  • Find by text starts a search with what the person entered, and can search both areas' comments.
  • When Search is pressed with the search text empty, the search is refused and the person is told "Please enter search terms."
  • A phrase opened with a double quote runs until a later word ending in a double quote; a phrase never closed is dropped without telling the person, which widens the search.
  • When none of In title, In description and In comment is ticked, the search is refused with "At least one attribute must be searched.
Dropped: 1 action
  • Closes the dialog as cancelled when Escape is pressed Dismissing a window is a control detail; a search that is not started changes nothing.
