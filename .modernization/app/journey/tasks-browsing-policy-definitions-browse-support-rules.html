Why this way recommended

Start from a product and see which support rules cover it, or from a name, with the system working out coverage.

The outcome is to see which products the rules cover, and starting from the product answers that in fewer steps.

Trade-off: Reverse lookup by product is not in the legacy and needs coverage to be worked out.
