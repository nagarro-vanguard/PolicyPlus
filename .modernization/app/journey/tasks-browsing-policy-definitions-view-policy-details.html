Why this way recommended

The whole context of a policy is shown together, with gaps (orphaned category, missing support) flagged up front.

One view answers all three questions and exposes broken references immediately, with fewer steps.

Trade-off: A fuller first view that carries more at once.

The architecture, modernized

Rules kept and dropped: 3 kept, 0 dropped
  • When a policy sits in a category that exists, the category name is shown and the category-details button is enabled.
  • When a policy names a support definition that exists, its name is shown and the support-details button is enabled.
  • The policy details window names the section a policy applies to: "User or computer" when it applies to both, "Computer" when machine-only, "User" when user-only.
Data it touches: Policy definition set, Policy setting

  • Policy setting Owned by Policy State. Identified by policy name, area.
