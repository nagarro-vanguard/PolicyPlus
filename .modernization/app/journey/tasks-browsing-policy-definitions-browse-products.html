Why this way recommended

Each product opens into its full family of versions in one view, with details beside the selection.

Gets from product to any version and its details in three steps instead of five, keeping every ordering and heading rule.

Trade-off: The three separate lists become one structure that has to stay readable for large families.

The architecture, modernized

Rules kept and dropped: 5 kept, 0 dropped
  • The top-level products list shows every loaded product ordered by name, each with its number of children.
  • The major versions list shows the children of the selected top-level product ordered by version, with the heading naming that product; with no product selected it is empty and prompts the person to select one.
  • The minor versions list shows the children of the selected major version ordered by version, with the heading naming it; with no major version selected it is empty and prompts the person to select one.
  • Opening product details by double-click or Enter does nothing when no product is selected in that list.
  • Double-clicking a child revision in the product details window shows that child in the same window; with nothing selected, nothing happens.
Dropped: 1 action
  • Resizes the Name column so it fills the list beside the Version column. Column width is presentation of one kind of screen.

Business rules

  • The top-level products list shows every loaded product ordered by name, each with its number of children.
  • The major versions list shows the children of the selected top-level product ordered by version, with the heading naming that product; with no product selected it is empty and prompts the person to select one.
  • The minor versions list shows the children of the selected major version ordered by version, with the heading naming it; with no major version selected it is empty and prompts the person to select one.
  • Opening product details by double-click or Enter does nothing when no product is selected in that list.
  • Double-clicking a child revision in the product details window shows that child in the same window; with nothing selected, nothing happens.

    Arrow keys to move, Enter to open, Escape to close.
