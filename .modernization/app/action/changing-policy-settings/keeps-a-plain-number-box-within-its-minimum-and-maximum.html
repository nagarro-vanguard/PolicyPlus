Business rules along the way (1)

Dialog windows and their reactions
A plain number box keeps any text a whole-number reading accepts, including a leading sign or spaces; other text is replaced by the default. A number above the maximum or below the minimum is replaced by that limit. Numbers above 2,147,483,647 are replaced by the default even when the policy maximum is higher. Only typing is restricted to digits; pasted text is not. A negative number kept where the minimum is below zero is converted to an unsigned number when applied.
