Capabilities and screens

Every capability the analysis found, grouped by area. Open one for its screens, actions and rules.

Reading policy definitions

Get Microsoft's policy template files, look through what they define, and find the policy to change.

Browsing policy definitions11 screensLook through the loaded policy templates: categories, products, support rules, template files and policies, and open the details of each.Finding policies6 screensNarrow the policy list by filter and find policies by ID, text or registry key and value, then pick a result.Getting Microsoft policy definitions2 screensDownload Microsoft's current policy template package and unpack it into a folder the program can read.

Setting and storing policies

Choose where policies are kept, change settings or raw keys and values, and save them back to that place.

Changing policy settings5 screensSet a policy to Enabled, Disabled or Not configured with its options and comment, and move settings in or out as SPOL text.Choosing and saving policy sources4 screensPick where the user and computer policies are kept (POL file, registry, a user's registry hive, a user's Group Policy object, or the live system) and save changes back to them.Editing policy files directly5 screensChange the raw keys and values of a policy file, and bring registry files in or out of it.

Starting the program and its settings

Start the program with the last definitions folder and policy sources restored, and let the person choose the language of policy text.

Using the product2 screensProduct's own settings, such as the language of the policy text.

Building Policy Plus

For the people who maintain the program: stamp each build with its current Git version. Nobody using the program meets this.

Building and versioning Policy Plus1 ruleStamp the program with its current Git version when it is built.
