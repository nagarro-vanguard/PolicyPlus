kept 3. Get the policies that control the setting.

Matches key paths and value names without regard to case, with wildcards, prefixes and path parts as stated, and keeps a policy when one of its settings meets every term.

takes the place of today's 1

Why, and what it keeps

The matching rules stay exactly as the business defines them.

  • A value-name term must match the whole value name; "*" and "?" act as wildcards, so without them the name must be equal.
  • A key term containing a backslash but no wildcard matches policies whose key path starts with that text.
  • A key term containing "*" or "?" must match the whole key path as a wildcard pattern.
  • A policy matches a registry search when at least one registry value it reads or writes meets every term entered; matching ignores case.
  • A key term with no backslash and no wildcard matches policies having a key path with a part equal to that text.
