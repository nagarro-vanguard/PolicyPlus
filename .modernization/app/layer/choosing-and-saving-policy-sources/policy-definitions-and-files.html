Policy definitions and files

Reads the administrative template and comment files, joins them into policies, categories and products, and holds the data shapes those steps use. In Choosing and saving policy sources: 0 actions and 78 business rules. Part of its code is also used by other capabilities.

The computer counts as having the Group Policy infrastructure only when the number Windows gives for its edition equals one of 59 fixed codes in a list. The edition is asked for as Windows version 6.0, and the result of the call is not checked. When the call fails the edition stays 0, which is not in the list, so the computer counts as having none.
