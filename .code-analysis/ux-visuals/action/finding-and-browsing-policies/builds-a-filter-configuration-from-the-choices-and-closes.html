When the filter dialog is confirmed with 'Supported on' ticked, a product filter is created even if no product is ticked; an empty product list is a filter, not 'no restriction'. When it is unticked, no product filter is created and both matching options are dropped.
PolicyPlus/FilterOptions.vb:110 · Screens: FilterOptions
A product filter keeps a policy only when the ticked products meet its support definition: an entry naming a ticked product is met, and an entry naming a version range of a parent product is met when a ticked product lies in that range. With no product ticked, no entry is met, so only policies the blank-support option admits can pass.

The option 'Match policies with missing or blank support definitions' decides policies with no support information. Ticked, policies with no support definition, a blank one, or an entry naming no product pass the product filter. Unticked, they are refused.
