Working with policies

People find a policy, read its definition, choose its state and values, edit raw policy files, and manage the definition files the policies come from: loading, downloading and inspecting ADMX/ADML files, products and support rules. Together this is the program's core job of viewing and changing Windows Group Policy settings.

Capabilities in this group

Finding and browsing policies
Browse policies by category and find them by text, ID, registry location or filter.
7 screens · 40 actions · 139 rules
Managing policy definitions
Load, download and inspect the policy definition files (ADMX/ADML) and the products and support rules they declare.
13 screens · 44 actions · 133 rules
Setting policies
Choose a policy's state and option values and apply them to a computer, user or policy file, including SPOL text import and inspection.
8 screens · 41 actions · 189 rules
Editing policy files directly
Edit the raw keys and values of a policy (POL) file and import or export them as REG scripts.
12 screens · 36 actions · 141 rules
